From copy to chaos in 4 steps

Never run a script before? It takes about a minute.

Get an executor

Use one that supports HttpGet, on PC or mobile.

Copy the loader

Hit Copy on the hub card above.

Paste and execute

Join your game, paste it into the executor and press Execute.

Choose your game

The Vanta menu opens. Tap EXECUTE on Blade Ball or MM2.

Good to know

Quick answers.

Is it free?

Yes. The loader is free and has no key system.

A script stopped working. What now?

Games update and can break scripts. Re-copy the loader here, since it always points to the latest version.

Will I get banned?

Using scripts always carries risk, including account bans. Use an alt account if you’re worried, and run everything at your own risk.

Which executor should I use?

Any executor that supports HttpGet. Avoid ones that bundle adware.

Vanta Scripts is a fan project and is not affiliated with Roblox or any game. Use at your own risk.
